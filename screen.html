🖱️ DigitBot Screen Trader

Works like the MMG bot: it sits on the trading website, reads the price from the screen, and clicks Buy / Sell / Close for you, with the take-profit and stop-loss typed in. No API keys needed. For PC browsers (Chrome, Edge, Firefox).

1. Install (once)

  1. Install Tampermonkey for your browser (Chrome Web Store / Edge Add-ons / Firefox Add-ons).
  2. Click Install Screen Trader and press Install in Tampermonkey.
    Chrome/Edge: also turn on "Allow user scripts" (or Developer mode) for Tampermonkey in the extensions page.
  3. Open a supported page. The DigitBot Screen Trader panel appears on the right. Updates arrive automatically.
Pages: binance.com/…/futures/… and /trade/… (spot), demo.binance.com, tradingview.com/chart, web.metatrader.app, trade.mql5.com. (Binance blocks bookmarklets, so use Tampermonkey.)

2. First run: practice clicks

  1. Log in to a demo / paper account: Binance Demo Trading, TradingView Paper Trading, or an MT5 demo account in MT5 Web.
  2. On Binance set leverage to 3x or less (the bot refuses above 3x).
  3. Panel › 🎯 Setup › Test all: yellow boxes show what the bot found. Anything with ✗ or a box on the wrong thing: press Teach next to it and click that thing on the page. (Your click is caught, nothing is traded.)
  4. On Binance tick TP/SL once so the take-profit / stop-loss boxes show, then check them with Show.
  5. ✋ Manual › BUY: with Practice clicks ON (the default) the bot only highlights each step. When every highlight is right, turn Practice clicks OFF in ⚙️ Settings and try one small demo trade.
  6. 🤖 Auto: pick the timeframe and strategy. It runs the backtest first and only trades a strategy that PASSES (an untested one only on demo, if you tick it). Keep the tab open; it acts when each candle closes.

What it does on every trade

  • Size from your risk % (balance read from the page on Binance, or typed in Settings). Choose whether the size box is in coins, USDT or lots.
  • Types the take-profit and stop-loss into the platform with the order, so the platform protects it even if your PC sleeps. It refuses to trade if it can't find the stop-loss box.
  • Then it moves its own stop to break-even at +1R and trails it, and clicks Close when that is hit.
  • Checks the position really opened (Binance "Positions(n)"); if not, it stops auto and warns you.
  • Daily loss limit, max trades per day, one trade at a time, max 3x leverage. REAL money needs you to type I UNDERSTAND.
  • Forex on TradingView / MT5 Web: history comes from Binance's matching pairs (EUR/USDT, GBP/USDT, AUD/USDT, PAXG for gold) and the live price from your screen.
Honest notes: screen bots depend on the page layout. If a site changes, the bot may not find a button; it then refuses and tells you, and you press Teach again. Keep the tab visible and the PC awake. Results shown in the panel are estimates from the screen price; the platform's own history is the true record. No strategy has passed the backtest on the markets tested so far, so expect Auto to refuse most of the time. Most traders lose money, and leverage makes losses fast. Nothing here is financial advice.
