How to use it

  1. Pick a market, timeframe and (crypto) Spot or Futures. The chart is live: it moves with every trade on Binance. Tick the indicators you want above the chart: EMAs, Bollinger Bands, volume, RSI, MACD, signals and your trades.
  2. 📈 Signals shows the market reading and the latest signal of each strategy, with its stop-loss and take-profit.
  3. 🧪 Backtest tests all 5 strategies on history. Only consider a strategy that says PASS on this market and timeframe.
  4. 🤖 Practice trading: start auto trading with a PASS strategy, or trade by hand, with $10,000 practice money. Practise for weeks (50+ trades) and compare with the backtest before you think about real money.
  5. ⚡ Live trading (PC) appears when you open this page from DigitBot.exe. It trades Binance Futures/Spot (testnet first) and forex/gold through MetaTrader 5 on a DEMO account: choose the market, press Save, then use Manual or Start auto. For MT5, set "Prices from" to MetaTrader 5 so the chart shows your broker's own prices.
  6. Timeframes: ⚡ Live = 1-second candles (crypto), then 1, 3, 5, 15, 30 minutes, 1 and 4 hours, 1 day. Very short timeframes are noisy: backtest before trusting them.
  7. 🔎 Search (top, or press /): find any Binance coin, forex pair, metal or energy market by name or symbol, e.g. "sol", "gold", "oil", "lira". ⚙️ Settings: chart colours, time zone, start-up view and timeframe, strategy defaults, practice balance, alert sound, auto-refresh.
  8. Side panel: 📊 the latest signal of every strategy, 🔔 price and signal alerts (sound + pop-up), 👀 live prices of other markets (click one to open it). ⛶ Full screen and ⤢ Bigger chart are above the chart; View › Mini shows just the chart and signals.
  9. 🖱️ Screen mode: prefer the bot to read the screen and click Buy / Sell on Binance, TradingView or MetaTrader 5 Web, like the MMG bot? Install the DigitBot Screen Trader.
  10. Real trading: the DigitBot PC app trades forex through MetaTrader 5 and crypto through Binance (testnet first), with the same strategies and limits.

The 5 strategies

Words

  • ATR: the average size of a bar; stops are set in ATRs so they fit how much the market moves.
  • R: the amount you risk on a trade. +2R = won twice what you risked.
  • PF (profit factor): money won ÷ money lost. Above 1 = profitable.
  • Max DD: the worst fall from a peak, in %.
  • Break-even: once a trade is +1R, the stop moves to the entry price.
  • Trailing stop: after break-even the stop follows the price at a distance of 2 × ATR.
Honest notes: a signal is a reading of the chart, not a promise. Backtests use past data; the future can differ. Forex prices on this page come from Binance pairs that follow the real rates (EUR/USDT ≈ EUR/USD, PAXG ≈ gold); your broker's prices differ slightly. Most retail traders lose money, and leverage makes losses fast. Never trade money you need.
