PC (Chrome / Edge / Firefox)
Easiest: show the bookmarks bar (Ctrl+Shift+B) and drag this green button onto it:

If you're signed into Chrome on your PC and phone with the same Google account, the bookmark appears on the phone by itself (bookmark sync).
Android (Chrome)
  1. Tap ⋮ → ☆ to bookmark this page, then tap Edit.
  2. Name: Digit Bot. Delete the URL and paste the copied code. Save.
iPhone / iPad (Safari)
  1. Tap Share → Add Bookmark → Save.
  2. Open Bookmarks → Edit → tap the new bookmark.
  3. Name: Digit Bot. Replace the address with the copied code. Done.

3. Use it

  1. Open mmgpredictions.com and log in.
  2. PC: click the Digit Bot bookmark.
    Android: tap the address bar, type Digit Bot and tap the bookmark in the suggestions (opening it from the bookmarks list doesn't work on Android).
    iPhone: open Bookmarks and tap Digit Bot.
  3. The Digit Bot panel appears on the game page.

Optional: open the bot automatically (no bookmark tap)

iPhone / iPad (Safari)
  1. Install the free app Userscripts from the App Store and open it once.
  2. iPhone Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions → Userscripts: turn it on and set mmgpredictions.com to Allow.
  3. In Safari open this script link, tap the aA / puzzle button → Userscripts → Install.
  4. Done: every time you open the game in Safari, the bot appears by itself and updates itself.
Android: Firefox + the Tampermonkey add-on, then open the same script link and tap Install.
PC: Chrome/Edge + Tampermonkey, then open the script link and tap Install.
Floating bubble: tap – on the bot to shrink it to a small bubble showing LIVE, trades and P/L. Drag it anywhere; tap it to open. The screen stays on while it runs.
